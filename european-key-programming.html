Common questions

Why is a European car key harder to replace?

Many European makers use encrypted immobilizer systems that need special equipment and security access to program. That's the work we focus on.

What does NASTF certified mean?

NASTF is the industry program that gives vetted locksmiths secure access to vehicle security information, such as key codes, that's normally limited to dealers.

Can you help if I lost every key to my BMW or Mercedes?

In many cases, yes. Call with your year, model and location and we'll tell you what's possible for your vehicle.

Do you come to me?

Yes. We're fully mobile across west St. Louis County and the surrounding area.
